Emilios Economic Reform Plans 1992
1. National Infrastructure Spine Program
What it is:
 Multi-year push to fix the core arteries:
Ports: Reunion City, Torrental, one Dalmatian port.


Roads: main industrial and rural spines.


Rail: trunk corridors prepared for electrification, diesel upgrades elsewhere.


Grid: substations and lines to stabilize power for industry and cities.


Status:
Moved through Parliament to presidents desk



2. Lucania Twin-Reactor “Trident” Nuclear Refit
What it is:
Refit and reopen the abandoned twin-reactor Lucania plant with modern safety systems.


Use it as the baseload anchor for electrification and industrial growth in the north.


Linked decision:
Capital nuclear plant in Reunion City is paused. Land and plans reserved, but no new build until Lucania proves the model.


Status:
Moving forward. Policy line agreed: pause capital plant, prioritize Lucania refit. Detailed engineering and financing planning underway.



3. Rural Modernization & Food Security Act (RMFS) – Draft Bill
What it is:
Equipment modernization grants + cheap credit for farmers.


Region-specific crop subsidies with “insurance” style reimbursements if harvests fail.


State purchase of surplus crops for strategic reserves and school/military supply.


Co-ops and storage (silos, cold chain) to cut waste and strengthen bargaining power.


Status:
Needs further planning.


Internally drafted with Agriculture, not introduced in Parliament yet.


Next steps: quiet consultations with farmers’ unions, co-ops, LUP, rural MPs before it goes public.



4. Land Tenure & Rural Property Modernization (Land Reform)
What it is:
National cadastre and title cleanup (starting in Lucania, Northern Island, Torrental hinterland).


Incentives for voluntary consolidation/co-ops (scale without forced seizures).


Tax pressure + incentives to force big underused estates to either modernize or partner with tenants.


Status:
Concept / white paper stage.


Emilio has asked Agriculture to work with him; no bill text yet.


This will be politically explosive if rushed – it needs precise legal work and a long rollout plan.



5. Rail Modernization & HS Trunk Line
What it is:
Kill steam across the network.


High-speed electric corridor:
 Lucania → Steinberg → Reunion City → Torrental (southern capital)
 – prioritized for speed, reliability, and affordable mass transport.


Diesel mainline/branch upgrades everywhere else (northern rural lines, Torrental hinterland, Dalmatia, fringe islands).


Status:
Strategic plan in development.


Political direction is set (this is the model).


Needs a formal 10-year National Rail Modernization Plan: exact km, costs per phase, contractor mix, and tie-in with Lucania grid timeline.



6. Government Expenditure Audit & Cuts (Confidential)
What it is:
Full audit of state spending to find:


Dead security programs and wartime leftovers.


Zombie SOEs and pointless subsidies.


Vanity projects and overlapping ministries.


Goal: free 2–4% of the budget over a few years to fund the infrastructure, nuclear, and rural programs without blowing the deficit sky-high.


Status:
Underway / For President’s eyes only.


Emilio has ordered it; Finance + audit bodies are compiling.


Output will be a confidential cut list for Donahue, then a public-facing “reform” package after they decide what to kill.
