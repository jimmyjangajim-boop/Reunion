Reunion Navy circa 73~
1 Battleship - Retired and made into a museum ship and sometimes a training vessel for larger vessels. Stripped of armaments. 
8 Cruisers - Four have been retrofitted into full blown missile cruisers modeled off of the Ticonderoga classes, with state of the art missile systems but notably being unreliable due to strain. Two were stripped of most araments and given the best communications and decetion equipment available and fitted to carry helicopters, turning them into portable command stations of Reunion design. The rest were scrapped.
20 Destroyers - 3 were retrofitted for training purposed, all other 17 were scrapped
35 Repair Ships - Majority sold or scrapped 
8 Submarines - Retired and all scrapped
