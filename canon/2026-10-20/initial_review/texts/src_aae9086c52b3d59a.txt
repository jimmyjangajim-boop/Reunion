[1] Emilios Economic Reform Plans 1992
[2] 1. National Infrastructure Spine Program
[3] What it is:
 Multi-year push to fix the core arteries:
[4] Ports: Reunion City, Torrental, one Dalmatian port.
[5] Roads: main industrial and rural spines.
[6] Rail: trunk corridors prepared for electrification, diesel upgrades elsewhere.
[7] Grid: substations and lines to stabilize power for industry and cities.
[8] Status:
[9] Moved through Parliament to presidents desk
[11] 2. Lucania Twin-Reactor “Trident” Nuclear Refit
[12] What it is:
[13] Refit and reopen the abandoned twin-reactor Lucania plant with modern safety systems.
[14] Use it as the baseload anchor for electrification and industrial growth in the north.
[15] Linked decision:
[16] Capital nuclear plant in Reunion City is paused. Land and plans reserved, but no new build until Lucania proves the model.
[17] Status:
[18] Moving forward. Policy line agreed: pause capital plant, prioritize Lucania refit. Detailed engineering and financing planning underway.
[20] 3. Rural Modernization & Food Security Act (RMFS) – Draft Bill
[21] What it is:
[22] Equipment modernization grants + cheap credit for farmers.
[23] Region-specific crop subsidies with “insurance” style reimbursements if harvests fail.
[24] State purchase of surplus crops for strategic reserves and school/military supply.
[25] Co-ops and storage (silos, cold chain) to cut waste and strengthen bargaining power.
[26] Status:
[27] Needs further planning.
[28] Internally drafted with Agriculture, not introduced in Parliament yet.
[29] Next steps: quiet consultations with farmers’ unions, co-ops, LUP, rural MPs before it goes public.
[31] 4. Land Tenure & Rural Property Modernization (Land Reform)
[32] What it is:
[33] National cadastre and title cleanup (starting in Lucania, Northern Island, Torrental hinterland).
[34] Incentives for voluntary consolidation/co-ops (scale without forced seizures).
[35] Tax pressure + incentives to force big underused estates to either modernize or partner with tenants.
[36] Status:
[37] Concept / white paper stage.
[38] Emilio has asked Agriculture to work with him; no bill text yet.
[39] This will be politically explosive if rushed – it needs precise legal work and a long rollout plan.
[41] 5. Rail Modernization & HS Trunk Line
[42] What it is:
[43] Kill steam across the network.

 Lucania → Steinberg → Reunion City → Torrental (southern capital)
 – prioritized for speed, reliability, and affordable mass transport.
[45] Diesel mainline/branch upgrades everywhere else (northern rural lines, Torrental hinterland, Dalmatia, fringe islands).
[46] Status:
[47] Strategic plan in development.
[48] Political direction is set (this is the model).
[49] Needs a formal 10-year National Rail Modernization Plan: exact km, costs per phase, contractor mix, and tie-in with Lucania grid timeline.
