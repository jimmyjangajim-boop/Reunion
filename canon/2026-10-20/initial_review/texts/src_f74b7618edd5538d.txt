RETREAT RULES

A roll of 6, 1-6, 1 being the worst possible outcome

RETREAT IN GOOD ORDER  +3
RETREAT +2
SHATTERED RETREAT -2

each die is worth 20 seconds of preparation and represents how low your mens morale in the said battle is

IF YOU ROLL A 3 OR UNDER YOUR MEN WILL NEED TIME TO EITHER REPLENISH THEIR MORALE AND NUMBERS DEPENDING ON SUPPLY LINES OR THEY WILL HAVE TO ROLL LOWER NUMBERS PER RETREAT

DICE ROLLS ARE REQUIRED TO CHECK YOUR MORALE

TROOP REPLENISHMENT IS BASED ON INFRASTRUCTURE AND MANPOWER AMONG SUPPLY AND OTHER THINGS

A DICE ROLL THROUGH 10 IS REQUIRED TO SEE IF YOUR TROOPS ARE REPLENISHED AND MORALE, THIS IS SOLELY AN RP ROLL AND SHOWS HOW MANY MEN YOU MIGHT BE ABLE TO PULL OUT, IF YOU GIVE YOUR ARMY TIME TO WAIT, THE ENEMY GAINS THE INITIATIVE BUT YOU REPLENISH MEN AND MORALE

LOW SUPPLY MEANS LOW MORALE, AND ATTRITION


ATTRITION

ATTRITION CAN BE CALCULATED BY A DICE ROLL OF 1-20, ATTRITION MODIFIERS CAN BE DISCUSSED DEPENDING ON SUPPLY LINES AND OTHER THINGS, INCLUDING AIR SUPERIORITY, SHELLING, BEING DUG IN, AND MORE (edited)
