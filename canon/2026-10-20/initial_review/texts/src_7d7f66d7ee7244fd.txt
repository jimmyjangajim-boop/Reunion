[1] Reunion Broadcasting Daily Network
[2] Special Report — Dalmatia Front Crisis
[3] Dateline: Reunion City | 10 September 1992 | 12:10 PM
[4] The Ministry of Defense confirmed today that Reunion forces in occupied Dalmatia have suffered severe losses following a major Croatian escalation that began with massed rocket artillery and 155mm shelling, followed by armored pressure and a breakthrough in the northwest sector.
[5] Officials say Croatian regular units—supported by UDF militia elements—successfully ruptured the line across the landbridge and pushed south of the lake beyond it, forcing Reunion units to fall back to a secondary defensive line. The withdrawal has effectively split parts of the force, with commanders reporting frayed communications and widespread disorder under sustained fire.
[6] Confirmed Losses (Reunion)
[7] According to the Defense Ministry’s preliminary list of equipment losses as of 11:54 AM, Reunion has lost:
[8] 12 155mm howitzers
[9] 3 AMX-13/90 light tanks
[10] 1 EC-130Q TACAMO aircraft
[11] 1 Mirage 5F, 2 Mirage IIIE, 1 Mirage IIIR
[12] 84 infantry sections (counted in this report as platoon-equivalents in the field returns)
[13] The government emphasized that casualty figures remain provisional while missing personnel are accounted for and scattered units are reassembled.
[14] Casualties (Platoon-based accounting)
[15] Under the Army’s current emergency reporting method—treating each listed infantry “section” loss as a 30-man platoon rendered combat-ineffective—the confirmed platoon-equivalent losses amount to:
[16] 84 platoons × 30 = 2,520 personnel (dead, wounded, missing, or captured)
[17] A senior defense official, speaking on background, described the pattern of losses as consistent with heavy rocket artillery strikes followed by break-ins and local overrun events.
[18] Croatian & UDF Losses (as reported by RNIO field assessment)
[19] Reunion claims significant Croatian losses including:
[20] 18 2A36 Giatsint-B 152mm guns
[21] 18 BM-21 Grad launchers
[22] 4 T-80 tanks
[23] One Croatian jet (MiG-19)
 and multiple infantry and support vehicles. UDF militia elements reportedly lost mortars and armored vehicles.
[24] Political Response
[25] President Donahue has convened an emergency national security session and requested urgent consultations with allied governments. Parliament is expected to be briefed today on emergency appropriations, mobilization measures, and internal security steps.
[26] RBD will continue updates as official casualty notifications and unit status reports are confirmed.
