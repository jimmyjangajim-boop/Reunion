The Royalist Party of Reunion -  (Far-Right-Reactionary) - This Reactionary Party was formed in 1946 shortly after the Civil war ended and the Monarchy lost its power. The unpopularity of the monarchy led to this party going underground. The party grew heavily in popularity following the 1968 Trident Nuclear Facility near meltdown. The party grew in popularity in 1973 after documents where published proving the Hartmann party gambled heavily on their actions during the Greek war, which could have backfired hard. The party grew far larger when in 1975, documents leaked proving the Hartmann party was performing insider trading and bribery among other corrupt actions. The Reactionary Party believes in remaining a democracy, but giving the king more power and returning the nation to its glory days (edited)

The Socialist Party of Reunion (Socialist-Leftist) - The Socialist Party takes its roots back to 1926 in Reunion when The Socialist Party of Torrental was formed. The Socialist Party of Torrental was a group of Reunionite Socialists who sought a revolution against the monarchy. The Party went on to join the 1943 Disco Civil War, siding with the Democratic Reunion Party. The Socialists formed The Socialist Party of Reunion in 1956 after going quiet for a few years. The Socialists believe in very common socialist ideas

The Reunion Revolutionary Front (Communist-Far-Left) - The Reunion Revolutionary Front branched off of the Socialist Party of Torrental in 1929 after the council had a near split due to different beliefs. The Revolutionary front actively believes in Marxist-Leninist communism and pushed for the Civil War. During the 1943 Disco Civil War, The Revolutionary Front supported with thousands of men, spies, weapons manufacturing, and propaganda (However the propaganda was communist in nature and wasn't very effective). The Revolutionary Front leadership where among the few men killed during the trial following the end of the civil war, and the party fell into obscurity until 1974 when the Reunion Senate, which was majority controlled by the Hartmann party, decided to accept bribes from the Revolutionary Front which let them operate freely as long as they paid a fee (Communism was and is still illegal in Reunion). Currently the Revolutionary Front pushes for a second civil war, promoting propaganda making Reunion seem like a racist, class oriented hellscape where only rich Disconians are allowed to suceed, while the poor struggle. Conservative Party (Conservative - Right) Shortly established after the end of the civil war, they became the first new party to come into power. The party first ran under the leadership of war hero Chester R. McPeak, who appealed to those of more traditional and the right leaning, despite his more liberal ideals. Chester lead the Conservative party to be that of a moderate party. The Conservative party would form a coalition with the Royalist Party of Reunion to secure a close election against the Hartman Party. Chester would lead for two terms before declaring his sudden retirement from politics. Their sudden drop in popularity was unable to challenge the Hartman party until the Trident Nuclear Scandal and the release of the Trident Papers (See History Channel). 

The Conservative party is a moderate party that prioritizes domestic production, neutrality, and domestic issues relating to the economy.

Hartman Party (Left) - The Hartman Party (Also known as the love party) traces it's origins back to the ATG, before the civil war. The party was formed after the civil war ended and was lead by prominent rebel leader Ebin Hartman. Ebin was a skilled politician, author, and lawyer among other things; his leadership skills were keen and the nation saw much needed reform during his three term rule, which passed with relative ease due to his status and the Hartman holding majority rule in the parliament and minor houses. During his third term Hartman and the party would face the Trident Scandal, which would cause Hartman to step down from the presidency before accusations could be thrown at him. Many viewed Hartman as a responsible figure for doing this, others not so much, but the effects would lead to the Hartman party loosing the next year to the Conservatives.

The Hartman Party is a let leaning group that seeks to mend domestic racial tension, expand on women's rights, and expand Reunion's influence.

The Royalist Party of Reunion - (Far-Right - Reactionary) - Technically the oldest party, once ruling Reunion in an absolute monarchy and forced to concede to a constitutional monarchy.  The party saw success in allying themselves to the conservatives to win the first election with the conservatives, but saw their popularity lower. Their falling has left them only to rule about 1/5 of the seats in the Parliament. Their recent resurgence come from the rekindling of the coalition formed many moons ago and their wants to expand the influence of the nation have drawn many of the left to support their cause. Their most prominant for the current president of the nation, President Stervo, a loyal member of the royalist party and democrat. 

The Royalist Party of Reunion seeks to keep the Royal family around, and restore glory. Their policies and beliefs are constantly changing due to the crowds they draw in.
