What is Horizon Dynamics?

Horizon Dynamics is a Reunion-based defense and industrial conglomerate led by Emilio Curro. The company was established through the strategic consolidation and acquisition of several underperforming military-industrial firms during a period of stagnation in Reunion’s defense production sector.
Notably, Horizon Dynamics has integrated multiple aerospace manufacturers into its portfolio, along with the revival of Lucania Motorworks, which has recently resumed production under new management.

What is the goal of Horizon Dynamics?

Horizon Dynamics is committed to reestablishing Reunion as a competitive force in advanced defense manufacturing. The company’s primary objective is the development and production of domestically manufactured aircraft for the Reunion Air Force, while also positioning itself as a supplier to key Western defense markets.
In parallel, Horizon Dynamics aims to address growing demand for high-performance, fuel-efficient military vehicles, with a particular focus on next-generation transport and logistics platforms.

What is Horizon Dynamics currently pursuing?

Horizon Dynamics is presently undergoing a comprehensive restructuring phase while initiating the development of three major operational campuses:
Reunion — Aerospace engineering and advanced aircraft development
Lucania — Vehicle engineering and automotive production
Split — Large-scale manufacturing and industrial fabrication

Following the establishment of these facilities, the company intends to acquire and develop a network of large-scale airfields to support testing, production, and deployment operations. These efforts will culminate in the development of the company’s flagship aerospace platform, the Harpye.

HD6x6L “Mule”
Light 6×6 logistics truck.
Loosely based on the KamAZ-4350.
Design philosophy: speed relative to heavier trucks, high reliability, excellent off-road mobility, and minimal complexity. It’s meant to move supplies where roads are bad or nonexistent and keep moving with minimal maintenance. Think “always starts, always gets through.”

HD6x6H “Ox”
Heavy 6×6 cargo truck, sister platform to the Mule.
Based on an enlarged, reinforced KamAZ-4350 concept rather than a totally new vehicle.
Design philosophy: payload over speed. Extended cab and bed, much stronger engine and drivetrain, optimized for traction and sustained movement under heavy load. This is the workhorse that doesn’t rush but doesn’t stop.

HD8x8VH "Herakles"
Heavy Modular Platform (KZKT-7428–class)
Large-scale heavy utility vehicle inspired by the KZKT-7428.
Design philosophy: brute utility and versatility. Not elegant, not fast, but extremely adaptable. Designed from the start as a modular platform (edited)
