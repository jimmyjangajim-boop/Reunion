[6] 1. SITUATION SUMMARY
[7] The island remains in a tense, localized standoff. Turkish forces hold the airfield terminal and immediate perimeter but have been driven off the radar ridge. The radar installation is completely destroyed (initial Tomahawk strike + Greek engineer demolition). The airfield runway is cratered and unusable for fixed-wing operations. No air superiority exists for either side following yesterday’s dogfight.
[8] Greek forces have established positions around the island and are actively probing our remaining lodgment. Their QRF (G2) is fully committed and has received no further reinforcement due to the mutual escalation standoff. Greek Follow-on (G3) remains at full strength but has not yet committed in force.
[9] No resupply has reached either side since the initial insertion. Ammunition, medical supplies, and food are critically low across all Turkish units.
[10] 2. BATTLE REPORT (Chronological)
[11] 06 Oct, 20:00 – Insertion: Heliborne assault begins. Alpha Platoon suffers catastrophic losses during a misplaced landing at the airfield. Two full squads are wiped out in the opening minutes by concentrated Greek security fire.
[12] 07 Oct, ~04:00: Airport terminal and immediate perimeter secured despite heavy casualties. Company Commander lands at first light and assumes command.
[13] 07–08 Oct: Forces dig in and consolidate. Plans for rapid reinforcement are placed on hold due to mutual threats of escalation. Greek QRF (G2) arrives by boat at ~01:30 on 07 Oct (1.5 hours post-touchdown) but receives no additional support.
[14] 09 Oct (Day 3): Three Greek squads supported by a BTR-40 and four mortars launch a determined assault on the radar ridge. After a 5-hour gunfight, Turkish forces are driven off the hill. Two heroic soldiers from Bravo Platoon remain in their sandbag positions, delaying the Greeks long enough for survivors to link up with the road defense squad. Both men are killed in place.
[15] Night of 09–10 Oct (Night 3): Greek forces (~80 troops) launch a heavy night assault on the airport terminal, supported by BTR-40, mortar fire, and the BMP-1. Company Commander is killed by an RPG detonation while personally defending the terminal. New Second-in-Command assumes command. BMP-1 is destroyed by a TOW/rocket hit at close range while attempting to withdraw at first light.
[16] 10 Oct: Greek aircraft attempt to regain air superiority. Dogfight results in Turkey losing 2 F-16s and Greece losing 1 Su-27. Both sides withdraw from direct overflight of the island. Airfield is further cratered by Greek close air support.
[17] Night of 10–11 Oct: Renewed heavy Greek assault on the airport perimeter (nearly 80 troops, BTR, and mortar support). Alpha Platoon is cut down severely and loses most forward positions by morning. Bravo Platoon is currently tasked with eliminating the Greek mortar position on the ridge but reports extremely low ammunition.
[18] The fight has remained strictly localized to the island as per High Command orders. No naval or mainland strikes have occurred.
