General lore:
Kingdom of Disco dates back to the early Roman Empire days when romans settled the area after taking it over from Greece. The land stayed with the Romans until the collapse of the empire when the Byzantines would take over. The Byzantines would also eventually lose the territory after the people rose up against their rule. The Kingdom slayed the Byzantines in the famed battle of Reunion's peak, when the Byzantine forces were defeated by king Discorius. The territory remained independent even from the Ottomans after the king was able to decisively defeat the Ottomans multiple times, keeping the Ottomans away and letting the Kingdom build. The Kingdom become a dominant power in trading rivaling the the Venetians and even fighting them multiple ways. By 1689the kingdom would face its downfall after the battle of Torrential bay was lost to the Venetians, losing the Kingdom major islands it controlled. The Island would remain a neutral power and didn't see more action until WW1, when they assisted the Greeks in kicking the Ottomans out of the Balkans. In 1924, King Zo was crowned king after the late death of his father. Zo was only 16 when crowned but showed well discipline and was well loved by the people. Zo's rule would eventually tighten as the king grew more tyrannical. Zo would essentially lock the country into a dictatorship, changing the constitution to put him in total power. The country was very much split on this, with some seeing the rule as tyrannical and to put the king out of power, while others showed much support for the king. A large opposing party rose up and started actively working against the king. The group was called the ATG and essentially heightened tensions to new extremes. On December 15, 1937 ATG members Nash Donahue and Ebin Hartman sued the King. The case was ultimately lost by the ATG but the news was one of the major events that put the country on the verge of civil war. (edited)

On June 1st, 1938, the King announced his support with the Nazi government and the Axis after multiple visits with Hitler and Bennito and reportedly went extremely well. The Duo were supposedly great friends and exchanged many gifts.  On July 10th, 1943 King Zo announced that the ATG was herby banned and anyone supporting it were "traitors". Major members and Founders Ebin Hartman and Nash Donahue fled to Lucania, which hosted one of the biggest ATG bases and declared that the ATG must not be stopped and that "If blood is to be spilled for the people, and guns to be raised for change, then the people will fight." The very next day they raised their flags in rebellion and seized the town. The ATG enacted operation "Royal Blood" and began the revolution. by July 11th, 1943 the civil war had begun.
