[1] REPUBLIC OF REUNION
[2] REUNION NATIONAL INTELLIGENCE REORGANIZATION ACT, 1992
 (“RNIO Act 1992”)
[3] Passed by the Assembly on: [Date, e.g. 29 July 1992]
 Transmitted to the President on: [Date]
[5] SECTION 1 – SHORT TITLE AND PURPOSE
[6] This Act shall be cited as the Reunion National Intelligence Reorganization Act, 1992.
[7] The purposes of this Act are:
 a. To establish a unified, civilian Reunion National Intelligence Agency (RNIA) responsible for the collection, analysis, and dissemination of intelligence in defense of the Republic.
 b. To distinguish clearly between:
[8] Intelligence activities, and
[9] Domestic security and paramilitary functions.
 c. To reorganize the existing Reunion Intelligence Agency (RIA) and GRI so as to:
[10] Transfer intelligence functions to the RNIA, and
[11] Retain RIA as a separate National Guard–type domestic security and reserve force under law.
 d. To provide for democratic oversight, judicial safeguards, and accountability.
[13] SECTION 2 – DEFINITIONS
[14] For the purposes of this Act:
[15] “RNIA” means the Reunion National Intelligence Agency created under this Act.
[16] “RIA” means the Reunion Intelligence Agency in its reconstituted form as a national guard/security force as provided herein.
[17] “GRI” means the existing foreign and strategic intelligence body merged into the RNIA under this Act.
[18] “Intelligence activities” means the lawful collection, processing, analysis, and dissemination of information concerning:
[19] Foreign states,
[20] Non-state actors,
[21] Domestic threats to the constitutional order and national security,
 subject to this Act and applicable law.
[22] “Domestic operations” means any operations conducted within the territory of the Republic, including deployments of forces, patrols, arrests, or other coercive measures.
[24] SECTION 3 – ESTABLISHMENT OF THE RNIA
[25] There is hereby created a civilian agency to be known as the Reunion National Intelligence Agency (RNIA).
[26] The RNIA shall:
 a. Be responsible for strategic and operational intelligence concerning threats foreign and domestic;
 b. Operate under the constitutional authority of the President and the Assembly;
 c. Be prohibited from exercising arrest powers, conducting detentions, or engaging directly in kinetic operations, except in narrowly defined circumstances as provided by law and in cooperation with the appropriate security or police forces.
[27] The RNIA shall have the authority to:
 a. Collect intelligence by lawful means;
 b. Maintain liaison with foreign intelligence and security services with the authorization of the government;
 c. Provide threat assessments and intelligence briefings to:
[28] The President,
[29] The Council of Ministers,
[30] The Assembly’s designated oversight committee, and
[31] Other bodies as may be authorized by law.
[33] SECTION 4 – DIRECTOR OF THE RNIA
[34] The RNIA shall be headed by a Director, who shall:
 a. Be appointed by the President of the Republic;
 b. Be confirmed by a three-fifths majority of the Assembly;
 c. Serve a single term of seven (7) years, removable only for:
[35] Proven misconduct,
[36] Incapacity, or
[37] Grave violation of law, on a two-thirds vote of the Assembly.
[38] No person may be appointed Director who:
 a. Within the last five (5) years has held the position of Director of RIA or GRI;
 b. Is currently serving as a member of the Assembly or in any party leadership position;
 c. Holds any other office of profit or command in the armed forces.
[39] The Director shall:
 a. Be a non-partisan civil servant;
 b. Be bound to provide full and truthful information to the Assembly’s oversight committee and, where appropriate, to the courts.
[40] (This is the clause that enrages NSF quietly; it blocks their old RIA chief.)
[42] SECTION 5 – TRANSFER OF INTELLIGENCE FUNCTIONS
[43] All intelligence analysis, strategic assessment, and foreign intelligence collection functions of the RIA are hereby transferred to the RNIA.
[44] All functions, records, archives, and assets of the GRI are hereby:
 a. Dissolved as a separate entity, and
 b. Transferred to the RNIA, which shall assume all lawful rights and responsibilities of the GRI relevant to intelligence activities.
[45] Personnel:
 a. Intelligence-analysis, foreign-intel, and technical-collection personnel of RIA and GRI may be transferred to the RNIA under:
[46] Merit-based selection, and
[47] Security vetting consistent with this Act.
 b. Personnel primarily engaged in paramilitary or domestic operational roles shall not be transferred as intelligence officers, except following:
[48] Retraining, and
[49] Reassignment under RNIA’s civilian employment standards.
[51] SECTION 6 – RECONSTITUTION OF THE RIA AS NATIONAL GUARD FORCE
[52] The RIA is hereby reconstituted as a National Guard–type domestic security force, distinct from the RNIA.
[53] The reconstituted RIA shall:
 a. Operate under a dedicated ministry or under the Ministry of Defense as determined by subsequent legislation;
 b. Consist of:
[54] Units trained and equipped for domestic security operations, civil defense, and support to police and armed forces in times of emergency;
[55] Personnel recruited and trained specifically for domestic operations, not as general reserves.
 c. Be prohibited from:
[56] Conducting independent intelligence operations, except tactical information gathering inherent in security deployments;
[57] Maintaining its own strategic intelligence-analysis directorates separate from RNIA.
[58] Rules of engagement, jurisdiction, and accountability of the reconstituted RIA shall be further specified by secondary legislation consistent with:
[59] The Constitution,
[60] This Act, and
[61] Acts concerning police and armed forces.
[63] SECTION 7 – OVERSIGHT AND SAFEGUARDS
[64] A permanent Assembly Committee on Intelligence and Security Oversight (“the Committee”) is hereby established to:
 a. Receive regular reports from the Director of RNIA;
 b. Review the legality, proportionality, and necessity of RNIA’s activities;
 c. Conduct inquiries into alleged abuses or violations by RNIA officers.
[65] Composition of the Committee:
 a. Members shall be appointed proportionally from recognized blocs in the Assembly;
 b. The Chair shall not be from the same bloc as the sitting President;
 c. At least one seat shall be reserved for opposition parties with no ministerial portfolios.
[66] Judicial safeguards:
 a. Any domestic collection of communications, or intrusive surveillance upon citizens, shall require judicial authorization except in narrowly defined emergency conditions, with:
[67] Post-facto judicial review, and
[68] Mandatory reporting to the Committee.
 b. RNIA shall not maintain detention facilities, nor shall it interrogate detainees except in cooperation with lawful police or relevant authorities.
[70] SECTION 8 – TRANSITIONAL ARRANGEMENTS
[71] Within ninety (90) days of this Act’s entry into force, the government shall:
 a. Present to the Assembly a detailed transition plan for the transfer of assets and personnel from RIA and GRI to RNIA;
 b. Propose the initial Director of RNIA for confirmation.
[72] All existing laws concerning RIA and GRI shall remain in force to the extent they are not inconsistent with this Act until repealed, amended, or replaced.
[73] Any ongoing operations of RIA or GRI at the time this Act enters into force shall be:
 a. Reviewed by the Director-designate of RNIA;
 b. Either:
[74] Transferred to RNIA,
[75] Transferred to the reconstituted RIA, or
[76] Terminated, in accordance with law and national security needs.
[78] SECTION 9 – BUDGETARY PROVISIONS
[79] The RNIA shall have a separate budget line within the national budget, subject to:
 a. Annual approval by the Assembly;
 b. Confidential annexes where necessary for security, with oversight by the Committee.
[80] The reconstituted RIA shall likewise have a distinct budget, clearly separated from RNIA expenditures.
[81] No off-the-books funding, unreported accounts, or unregistered assets shall be permitted for RNIA or the reconstituted RIA. Any such schemes shall constitute criminal offenses under the Ethics & Conflict of Interest Act and related statutes.
[83] SECTION 10 – ENTRY INTO FORCE
[84] This Act shall enter into force upon signature by the President of the Republic and publication in the Official Gazette.
[85] The government shall adopt necessary implementing decrees and regulations within six (6) months of entry into force.
