[1] CONFIDENTIAL — EYES ONLY
 Via secure diplomatic channel
 From: Office of the President of Serbia
 To: President Dax Donahue II, Republic of Reunion
 Subject: Betrayal of Confidence — Bosnian Arms Scheme / Ploče Fallout
[2] President Donahue,
[3] I am writing in anger and in disbelief.
[4] Serbia has treated Reunion as a partner of consequence—one capable of discipline, statecraft, and keeping its own house in order. What we have learned in the last forty-eight hours makes that assumption look naïve.
[5] We now have credible intelligence indicating that your chief financial adviser was working with Bosnian actors, and that multiple figures of power in Reunion—not low-level smugglers, not “unauthorized patriots”—were engaged in a plan to arm Bosnia through covert channels. The structure of the plan is too detailed, too resourced, and too coordinated to be dismissed as rumor. There are names. There are routes. There are intermediaries. There are financial movements that do not occur by accident.
[6] Let me be clear: if Serbia is being used as a convenient friend to keep your state upright while elements inside your system play both sides—then you are gambling with a relationship you cannot afford to lose.
[7] What makes this worse is the timing and context. We were preparing our own arrangement with Bosnia—one based on hard realities, not sentiment. Bosnia abruptly backed out for reasons still unknown, and now the outline becomes visible: a full scheme laid out to move weapons, money, and equipment, with Reunion-linked actors in the picture. That is not “policy drift.” That is betrayal.
[8] You will understand why I am alarmed by what remains unknown. If the deeper relationship between Reunion power-brokers and Bosnian factions were to become public, it would suggest something far more corrosive: that powerful Reunionists were willing to tolerate—perhaps even enable—events like Ploče for their own purposes. I do not need you to confirm that for me in writing. I am telling you that Serbia will not be pulled into a fog where we end up carrying the blame for other people’s games.
[9] Here is what I require, immediately:
[10] A direct secure call within 12 hours with you personally—not an aide, not a minister—so I can hear plainly whether your government is in control of itself.
[11] Immediate suspension and isolation (administrative leave, access revoked, communications restricted) of any financial, intelligence, or logistics officials credibly named in this scheme—pending investigation.
[12] A written assurance, transmitted through official channels, that Reunion will not facilitate arms transfers to Bosnia through criminal syndicates, cut-outs, or state-connected “private” structures.
