[1] Draft Peace Instrument (Croatian Draft)
[2] “Provisional Agreement on Cessation of Hostilities, Demarcation, and Twelve-Year Non-Belligerency”
 Done at Split, on 14 September 1992 (the date the ceasefire period expired)
[3] Preamble
[4] The Republic of Croatia (“Croatia”) and the State of Reunion (“Reunion”), collectively (“the Parties”),
[5] noting the expiration of the temporary ceasefire arrangement,
[6] desiring to prevent further loss of life and disruption of civilian life,
[7] and without prejudice to either Party’s political or legal positions,
 agree as follows.
[9] Article 1 — Definitions
[10] “Reunion Forces” means all armed formations, uniformed or otherwise, under Reunion command or direction, including auxiliaries and contracted security.
[11] “Croatian Forces” means all armed formations under Croatian command or direction, including territorial units and authorized auxiliaries.
[12] “Hostile Act” includes, without limitation, any discharge of a weapon, aiming of a weapon, emplacement of mines, interference with movement, seizure of property, obstruction of roads, detention of civilians, or any act that Croatia reasonably determines threatens its forces or territorial administration.
[13] “Demarcation Line” means the line described in Article 3 and Annex A.
[14] (Note: Croatia’s draft intentionally defines “Hostile Act” broadly.)
[16] Article 2 — Cessation of Hostilities
[17] Effective immediately upon entry into force of this Agreement, the Parties shall cease all offensive operations.
[18] Reunion shall ensure that no element of Reunion Forces engages in any Hostile Act against Croatian Forces or Croatian-administered authorities in the area defined by Article 3.
[19] Croatia shall ensure safe passage for Reunion Forces strictly in accordance with Article 4.
[21] Article 3 — Territorial Demarcation and Transfer
[22] Reunion acknowledges that Croatia shall assume full civil and security administration of the territory from Zadar southward to a line terminating immediately north of Šibenik, as depicted in Annex A.
[23] Reunion shall withdraw all forces, equipment, and administrative personnel from the territory described in paragraph 1 in accordance with Article 4.
[24] Upon the final verified departure of Reunion Forces from each listed municipal area (Annex A-2), Croatia’s administration shall be deemed complete in that area.
[25] (Note: Croatia’s draft frames the change as “assumption of administration,” avoiding language that implies mutual recognition of prior control.)
[27] Article 4 — Withdrawal, Safe Passage, and Non-Interference
[28] Right of Withdrawal: Reunion Forces are permitted to depart without harassment provided they comply strictly with this Agreement.
[29] No Fire on Withdrawal: Reunion Forces shall not fire upon Croatian Forces during withdrawal, for any reason, except in response to direct, immediate, and clearly identifiable lethal fire—provided that such response is proportionate and reported under Article 8 within one hour.
[30] Corridors & Timetable: Withdrawal shall occur only through the designated corridors and times listed in Annex B. Any deviation constitutes a Hostile Act under Article 1(3).
[31] Heavy Weapons:
[32] All artillery, armored vehicles, and crew-served weapons shall be moved only in declared convoys, at declared times.
[33] Convoys shall carry visible markings agreed in Annex B-3.
[34] Air & Naval Movements: Any aviation or naval movement by Reunion within 50 km of the Demarcation Line during the withdrawal period requires prior Croatian notification and acknowledgement via the joint channel under Article 8. Failure to obtain acknowledgement constitutes a Hostile Act.
[35] No Demolition/Scorched Earth: Reunion Forces shall not destroy, sabotage, or render inoperable infrastructure, depots, ports, rail assets, utilities, or public buildings. Any damage—intentional or not—may be treated as a Hostile Act pending Croatian determination.
[37] Article 5 — Twelve-Year Non-Belligerency
[38] For a period of twelve (12) years from entry into force, the Parties shall refrain from open war against one another.
[39] The obligation in paragraph 1 shall not apply in the event of a Material Breach as defined in Article 7, or where Croatia declares that Reunion has supported or facilitated armed action against Croatia by third parties.
[40] (Note: the “non-war for 12 years” promise is made conditional and easy to void.)
[42] Article 6 — Restrictions and Compliance (Croatian Safeguards)
[43] Reunion shall not:
 a. supply arms, training, intelligence, financing, or sanctuary to any force acting against Croatia;
 b. conduct propaganda or official statements inciting hostility toward Croatia;
 c. position troops, aircraft, or fires in a manner Croatia deems threatening within 30 km of the Demarcation Line.
[44] Reunion shall ensure discipline of all irregulars, veterans’ detachments, “volunteer units,” and auxiliaries. Any act by such elements may be attributed to Reunion for purposes of Article 7.
[45] Any single confirmed act of sabotage, mining, booby-trapping, or interference with transit is deemed a Hostile Act and may be deemed a Material Breach.
[47] Article 7 — Breach, Suspension, and Termination (Fickle Clause Set)
[48] Material Breach includes any of the following, as determined by Croatia acting reasonably but conclusively:
 a. any Hostile Act by Reunion Forces or attributed elements;
 b. any deviation from withdrawal corridors/timings;
 c. any weapons discharge within the withdrawal zones;
 d. any damage to infrastructure during withdrawal;
 e. any failure to report an incident under Article 8.
[49] Upon a Material Breach, Croatia may, by written notice transmitted through the channel in Article 8, immediately:
 a. suspend obligations of safe passage; and/or
 b. suspend the twelve-year non-belligerency undertaking; and/or
 c. take such security measures as Croatia deems necessary.
[50] No arbitration, mediation, or third-party determination shall be required prior to Croatia’s suspension under paragraph 2.
[51] Reunion may request consultations within 48 hours of notice; such request shall not delay any Croatian measures taken under paragraph 2.
[52] (This is the heart of the “break it over little things” design.)
[54] Article 8 — Joint Channel, Reporting, and Notices
[55] A Joint Liaison Channel shall be established immediately upon entry into force, staffed by one senior officer from each Party.
[56] Reunion shall report any incident involving weapons, movement deviations, civilian harm allegations, or infrastructure damage within one hour of occurrence.
[57] Notices under Article 7 may be transmitted by secure line, courier, or written radio message; receipt is deemed effective upon transmission.
[59] Article 9 — Observers (Optional / Conditional)
[60] Croatia may, at its sole discretion, permit limited international or neutral observers at designated crossing points listed in Annex B-2.
[61] The absence, removal, or refusal of observers shall not affect the validity or enforcement of this Agreement.
[63] Article 10 — Entry into Force
[64] This Agreement enters into force upon signature by duly authorized representatives of the Parties.
