President Ebin Hartmann - Ebin was born in Steinberg in 1916 to a poor family that made most of their money from their farm. Not too much is known about his childhood besides the fact that he completed High School and was accepted into a University in Italy to continue his studies. By age 19 he had left the University to assist his ailing father in the farm, when he met a man named Nash Donahue who he was friend with in High School. The two shared similar ideas about government, both disliking how much direct power the king held. In 1935 they formed a group named the "Anti-Tyranical-Group" or the ATG. They would actively protest the King from their headquarters in Steinberg until one day, men from the Legio Equestris threatened to arrest members of the ATG in the headquarters. The ATG would refuse and The Legio Equestris began firing on the building, claiming the men inside where shooting back, which they where not. The building caught fire after the Legio Equestris began throwing Molotov's, and nearly 43 people died in the tragedy. Ebin and Nash where visiting Lucania with members of the ATG where they avoided certain death. This massacre caused Ebin and Nash to meet up with another anti-government faction based in Lucania and they spent 2 years gathering equipment via connections into local garrisons. 
"If blood is to be spilled for the people, and guns to be raised for change, then the people will fight." -Ebin Hartmann 
The very next day they raised their flags in rebellion and seized the town. The ATG enacted operation "Royal Blood" and began the revolution. by July 11th, 1943 the civil war had begun.

In 1943, the ATG gathered up and captured a munitions plant in Lucania. This sparked the Disco Civil War which ended in 1947.

Hartmann was pardoned following the ceasefire and went on to live in Reunion, working as a politician and assisting in reconstruction efforts. In 1973 he was eventually elected president following a long presidential campaign (edited)
