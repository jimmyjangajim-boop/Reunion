# Import notes for a future Codex build (not the build prompt)

Future system objective: a locally editable, source-linked Reunion encyclopedia and timeline with an explicit adjudication interface for conflicting records and undated events.

Priorities for the next conversation about the Codex prompt:
- A **simple conflict inbox** with side-by-side source snippets and buttons/choices for accept A, accept B, preserve both with context, supersede with edited value, or leave unresolved. Record decision history.
- A **missing-data inbox** that shows missing dates, unknown people, casualty definitions, unsourced claims, without fabricating answers. Support exact dates, uncertain spans, approximate and unknown.
- Easy status changes (canon, proposed, planned, simulated, ordered, delivered, operational, superseded, disputed) and user confirmation workflow.
- Source line citations in every dossier paragraph and ability to open the original text at the cited line.
- Deduplication across overlapping baselines while keeping raw originals intact.
- Date-aware snapshots rather than a timeless state; a war order in 1992 does not imply delivery by October 3.
- Imports from later TXT files, full ChatGPT chat exports, Discord channel exports, images and maps. Importing must be reversible, deduplicated and labeled by source.
- Automatic generation of readable Markdown/PDF dossiers only after canon adjudication; no automatic retcons.

Do not begin by rewriting every claim. Begin with ingestion, versioning, provenance and testable conflict detection.
